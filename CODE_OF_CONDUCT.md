# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project is for people who like small, well-made software, and everyone is welcome
regardless of experience, background or identity.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Assume good faith, give constructive feedback, and credit others' work.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the open.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat
offenders. To report a problem, contact the maintainer via the email on their GitHub profile.
